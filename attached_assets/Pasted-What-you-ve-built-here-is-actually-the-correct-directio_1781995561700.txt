⚠️ What you still need to be careful about

Even though this is good, there are 3 hidden risks:


---

1. ⚠️ In-memory cache limitation

You said:

> “no Redis needed”



This works ONLY for:

single server

low traffic

development


At scale:

memory resets on restart

multiple instances won’t share cache

500 RPM + scaling → inconsistent behavior


👉 Eventually you WILL need Redis.


---

2. ⚠️ Cache explosion risk

You are caching:

images

product names

fingerprints

AI responses


If uncontrolled:

> memory grows forever



You need:

TTL rules

eviction strategy (LRU / LFU)

size caps


Otherwise performance will degrade over time.


---

3. ⚠️ “False cache hits”

Fingerprint matching can fail when:

lighting changes image

packaging changes slightly

different resolutions


So you may get:

wrong reuse

incorrect nutrition mapping


👉 You need confidence scoring or fallback verification.
